How ordering works

Four simple steps

  • 1. Add to cart. Pick the part and the value or option you need.
  • 2. Send the order form. Name, phone number and pickup or delivery. No sign-up.
  • 3. We confirm. We call or WhatsApp you. Your order code lets you track the status.
  • 4. Collect and pay. 40% advance on order confirmation, and remaining balance (cash/UPI) on pickup or delivery.
FAQ

Questions students ask

How does payment work?

Once your order is confirmed, you pay a 40% advance via UPI to begin packing/assembly. The remaining 60% balance is paid on pickup or delivery (Cash or UPI).

How long does it take to get my order?

We confirm by call or WhatsApp after you order and tell you when it will be ready. Items that are in stock are usually ready quickly.

Are the parts new and genuine?

We sell new parts. Specs are listed on each product page. If something does not work, tell us within a few days with your order code and we will help you sort it out.

I need a value or part that is not listed.

Write it in the order notes when you check out, or message us on WhatsApp. We will tell you if we can get it.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes, before it is picked up or delivered. Contact us with your order code.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum order for pickup.

Safety note on LiPo batteries, mains and soldering

LiPo cells need a protected charger. Mains transformers carry dangerous voltage, so get supervision if you are a beginner. Solder in a ventilated area and never leave an iron on.

Need help choosing parts?

Message us with your project idea and we will suggest a parts list.
