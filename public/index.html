Quick answers

Good to know

How does payment work?

Send the order form. Once our team verifies stock and confirms your order, you pay a 40% advance via UPI to initiate packing/assembly. The remaining balance is paid on pickup or delivery (Cash / UPI).

What if I need a resistor or IC value that is not listed?

Write it in the order notes at checkout and we will confirm availability when we contact you.

How do I know my order is confirmed?

You get an order code right after ordering. We contact you on your phone to confirm, and you can check the status any time on the Track order page.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, before pickup or delivery. Message us on WhatsApp or call with your order code.
